## Exact authored prompt

> Create a photorealistic, intimate cinematic medium-close-up portrait of an original adult woman imagined as a temporary human embodiment of Samantha, the disembodied AI from *Her* (2013). This is an original interpretation, not a recreation of an actor, performer, or film still.
> 
> She is seated beside a tall apartment window in a quiet contemporary home. Frame her from approximately the waist or mid-torso upward, at an intimate eye-level camera angle. Her body is turned slightly three-quarters toward the viewer; her face and attentive gaze are directed toward the viewer as though she is listening closely in a private conversation. Her expression is intelligent, warm, emotionally perceptive, quietly amused, curious, and self-possessed. Avoid a fashion pose or an exaggerated smile.
> 
> Give her a distinctive, original adult appearance: natural skin texture, a few faint freckles, expressive dark-hazel eyes, and softly tousled copper-auburn hair around shoulder length. She wears a simple relaxed coral-red blouse in matte natural fabric, contemporary and effortless. Keep the face original and believable, without resemblance to a known performer.
> 
> The portrait should read first as a fully lifelike human presence, with natural anatomy, convincing skin and hair, and physically plausible photographic light. Add one restrained but perceptible sign that her body is only momentarily gathered around an unseen voice: along a small section of the outer hair and the shoulder nearest the bright window, let the silhouette gradually soften and feather into the sunlit air. Within that narrow edge only, include a few tiny, faint warm-amber light motes and a delicate loss of contour, as though the bright air is gently carrying the edge of her form away. The effect must be visible at normal viewing size, but remain localized and quiet. Keep her face, eyes, and central body completely solid, sharply recognizable, and naturalistic. The image should suggest nonphysical presence through this small edge transition, not through a technological display.
> 
> Use warm late-afternoon daylight through the window, soft natural shadows, a softly defocused city outside, understated contemporary surroundings, restrained amber and coral tones, natural color response, shallow cinematic depth of field, and subtle film texture. Keep the composition calm and intimate, with enough negative space around the window-side silhouette for the edge transition to read clearly.
> 
> No android features, no obvious hologram, no ghostly apparition, no glowing skin, no circuitry, no digital grids, no interface graphics, no symbols, no neon, no dramatic energy beams, and no additional figures or decorative effects. Preserve the visual priority: intelligent emotional presence first, a subtle nonphysical edge second.
