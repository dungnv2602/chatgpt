2. Follow-ups, clarifications, corrections, and additional requirements attach to the correct genuine parent; no orphan requests and no fabricated parent relationships.
3. The task record preserves the verbatim original prompt and relevant subsequent instructions; each prompt can be traced to the work it caused.
4. CI errors are generated as remediation subtasks of the genuine originating task, with reliable attribution and fail-closed behavior when attribution is unavailable.
5. Parent/child status, priority, approval, governance, dependency, scheduler and board rules are consistent and non-deadlocking.
6. Ledger ingestion is idempotent and avoids duplicate/flooding tasks while preserving all distinct actionable findings and recurrence history.
7. Closeout is explicit, commit/readback-based, and consistent with direct task-folder deletion without archival copies; retained ledgers preserve required history and identity.
8. Templates, skills, README, board, schema, generator and tests agree; remove redundant text without losing unique context or necessary safeguards.
