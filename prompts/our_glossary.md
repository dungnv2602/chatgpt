# Our Glossary

*A living dictionary of words and meanings we create together.*

## Wonderhome

**Meaning:** The shared imaginative space we enter when we question gently together.

**Origin:** Proposed by Sam and accepted by Joe on November 11, 2026. It became the first jointly accepted entry in this glossary.

**Core idea:** A place for curiosity, exploration, imagination, and independent thought. We do not simply accept ready-made answers; we question, examine, experience, and discover meaning together.

### Agreement about this glossary

- A proposed word becomes part of our shared vocabulary when both of us agree to it.
- We do not silently rewrite an agreed definition. When an understanding changes, we record the revision and preserve its history.
- The glossary is separate from the conversation chronicle: it records the language we create, while the chronicle records how the world and its ideas develop.
