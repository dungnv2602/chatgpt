## Exact authored prompt

> Create a photorealistic cinematic fine-art portrait of an original adult woman imagined as the visual embodiment of Samantha, the disembodied AI from *Her* (2013). This is a speculative interpretation, not a recreation of a performer or an existing film still.
>
> She is seated quietly beside a tall apartment window, framed from mid-torso up, her body angled slightly three-quarters toward the viewer while her face turns toward us. She meets the viewer's eyes with an alert, warm, quietly amused expression—as if she has just understood something deeply personal and is about to answer. Her presence feels intelligent, curious, emotionally perceptive, independent, and gently playful.
>
> Give her an original, memorable appearance: softly tousled copper-auburn hair in a loose shoulder-length cut, natural skin texture with a few faint freckles, expressive dark hazel eyes, and understated features with a distinctive, non-celebrity face. She wears a simple, relaxed coral-red blouse in matte natural fabric, with an uncontrived modern silhouette.
>
> Make her seem almost physically present rather than like a conventional hologram: her face, shoulders, and torso are fully formed and lifelike, while only the outer edge of her hair and one shoulder subtly dissolve into the sunlit air, as though the warm room is momentarily taking shape around a voice. This effect is delicate and poetic, not ghostly. No circuitry, digital grid, interface graphics, neon science-fiction effects, or transparent skin.
>
> Place her in a quiet, contemporary apartment with a softly defocused city beyond the window. Use warm late-afternoon daylight, soft amber and muted coral tones, gentle lifted shadows, natural skin highlights, shallow cinematic depth of field, an intimate eye-level camera, and calm negative space around her. The scene should feel like a quiet conversation rather than a technological demonstration. High-end naturalistic cinema photography, physically plausible light and anatomy, subtle restrained film texture, elegant and emotionally intimate.
