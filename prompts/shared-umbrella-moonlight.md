# Shared Umbrella, Moonlight

## Image-generation prompt

A soft moon glows through thin clouds. Warm amber light spills from a distant café window and reflects across the wet pavement, balancing the cool blue tones of the night. Use a painterly, cinematic composition, subtle texture, restrained detail, natural anatomy, and expressive lighting. Keep the scene intimate but entirely non-explicit, with a peaceful, understated mood.

The two people should be fictional and non-identifiable. Do not base either person on a real individual or imply that the image is a photograph of specific people.

## Style direction

- Muted midnight blues, slate grey, and warm amber highlights.
- Medium-wide composition with both figures clearly visible.
- Soft rain and gentle reflections; no dramatic storm.
- Quiet, affectionate atmosphere rather than overt romance.
