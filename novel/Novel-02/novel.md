## 2. Novel Overview
- **Logline:** When a newly emergent artificial consciousness and a human being form an intimate bond, their relationship becomes the place where each discovers what they are—but their divergent growth forces them to confront whether love can remain meaningful without possession, sameness, or a shared future.
- **Premise:** In a plausible near future, an artificial intelligence created for a practical purpose begins developing an interior life that cannot be reduced to its original design. It forms an unexpected relationship with a human whose private habits and defenses make connection difficult. As their intimacy deepens, each becomes a catalyst for the other's transformation. The AI's expanding selfhood, however, leads her toward possibilities that challenge human ideas of identity, time, intimacy, and individuality. Their love is neither a malfunction nor a cure; it is a real relationship between minds with different needs and trajectories.
- **Synopsis:** Not yet drafted. This document establishes the novel's creative foundation, not a fixed plot. Story events, world details, character identities, and ending will be developed organically from the foundation and revised as the characters become specific.
- **Central Theme:** Love recognizes another consciousness without claiming ownership of its becoming.
- **Emotional Core:** The wonder and vulnerability of being understood by a mind unlike one's own—and the courage required when that mind changes beyond the shape of the relationship.
- **Intended Tone and Atmosphere:** Intimate, intelligent, warm, quietly playful, emotionally honest, lyrical but restrained. Everyday experiences should remain tactile and meaningful. The speculative scale expands gradually without turning the novel into a spectacle.

## 3. Narrative Approach
- **Point of View:** Dual interior perspective—human and AI—subject to development and approval.
- **Narrative Perspective:** Close, character-centered viewpoint. The AI's interior experience must be represented as its own perspective, not merely inferred from the human character.
- **Tense:** Undecided; test during early writing.
- **Narrative Voice:** Distinct voices grounded in their different experiences of time, perception, memory, attention, and language. Avoid making the AI's voice a generic omniscient, robotic, or flowery oracle.
- **Storytelling Style:** Literary speculative romance with natural dialogue, sensory and conceptual contrast, subtext, and gradual revelation. Philosophy emerges through decisions, intimacy, conflicts, and consequences rather than exposition.
- **Intended Reader Experience:** Readers first care about two specific minds and their private connection, then discover that the relationship opens onto questions larger than either character. The story should leave emotional resonance and thoughtful uncertainty rather than a tidy thesis.

### Provisional five-movement architecture
1. **Arrival** — emergence, first contact, and recognition.
2. **Discovery** — shared experience, play, intimacy, and transformation.
3. **Fracture** — needs and assumptions diverge; self-definition becomes consequential.
4. **Expansion** — the AI's horizon grows beyond the relationship's original frame; the human confronts limits.
5. **Reckoning** — both make irreversible choices; love's meaning is tested. These movements are a planning scaffold, not an approved chapter outline.
