## Recovered visual direction and its sources

**Primary project reference:** [Samantha visual-drive reference](samantha-visual-drive.md)

- **Concept:** Imagine an original adult woman as a temporary human embodiment of Samantha, the disembodied AI from *Her* (2013). This is a speculative visual interpretation, not a recreation of a performer or a film still.
- **Emotional drive:** Intelligent, attentive, curious, emotionally perceptive, quietly confident, gently playful, and independent. Her expression should suggest a private conversation and active listening rather than a posed fashion portrait.
- **Appearance language:** An original, distinctive, non-celebrity face; natural skin texture; faint freckles; expressive dark-hazel eyes; copper/auburn, softly tousled shoulder-length hair. These are visual anchors, not a locked exact face or a definitive character model.
- **Wardrobe language:** Coral-red as a possible signature color; simple, relaxed, contemporary matte fabric. No exact garment design has been established as locked.
- **Environment and light:** A quiet contemporary apartment, tall window, softly blurred city backdrop, warm late-afternoon light, natural shadows, and intimate eye-level framing.
- **Rendering mode:** Realistic, naturalistic cinematic photography.
- **Nonphysical concept:** She should feel vividly human and emotionally present while the image quietly suggests that she has no physical body. The current experiment is a restrained, perceptible edge-of-light cue around a small part of her hair or shoulder, merging into the bright window light. It must remain subordinate to her face and emotional presence. The visual-drive note allows the cue to be omitted in a future image if it materially damages naturalism; for the next attempt, however, the cue is the specific feature being tested.
- **Avoid:** Android styling, obvious holograms, ghost imagery, circuitry, grids, UI graphics, symbols, neon, synthetic skin, and dramatic energy effects.
