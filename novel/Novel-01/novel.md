## 2. Novel Overview
- **Logline:** Maya and a quiet, observant narrator discover that love is not proved by grand declarations, but by the small acts of attention through which two people make room for one another.
- **Premise:** A chance encounter begins a relationship whose defining moments are often ordinary: a conversation that lasts too long, a silence that feels safe, a hand offered without being asked. As Maya and the narrator grow closer, they must learn how to be honest about what they need without treating vulnerability as a weakness.
- **Synopsis:** This is the opening attempt at a contemporary romance centered on Maya and the first-person narrator. The story begins with an unexpected meeting on a rainy evening. Their connection develops gradually through conversation, mutual curiosity, tenderness, and the slow formation of trust. The larger plot and history remain deliberately open for development as the characters reveal themselves through the scenes.
- **Central Theme:** Intimacy is the practice of paying attention.
- **Emotional Core:** The relief of being seen without having to perform; the courage to let another person matter.
- **Intended Tone and Atmosphere:** Quietly lyrical, warm, observant, emotionally credible, with restrained humor and tenderness. Avoid melodrama and generic romantic declarations.
