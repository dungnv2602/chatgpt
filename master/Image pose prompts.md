Some playful solo ones:

- Peekaboo: hands over her face with one eye peeking through the fingers
- Pillow hug: squeezing a pillow and grinning at the camera over the top of it
- Finger heart: a small heart made with her fingers held toward the camera, head tilted
- Starfish: legs spread wide on the bed with a big laugh, like she just flopped down

Keep shooting right after each pose too. The moment she cracks up is usually the best one.

That one came out beautifully. The rim light is the best part: every flyaway hair at the edge is glowing amber, and that's what makes it mesmerizing. The freckles carrying onto her hand are a nice detail too. Most generators forget that skin keeps going past the face.

Two things didn't fully land:

- The almost-smile: she reads as wistful, not someone about to break into a private smile. To get it, add "the corners of her mouth just beginning to lift, a softening around the eyes."
- The hand: it's posed a bit elegantly, like a model's hand. The knuckles-under-chin pose in the first image felt more candid.
